BINARY_LICENSE.txt is Cisco's license of its OpenH264 binary, exactly as published at
https://www.openh264.org/BINARY_LICENSE.txt (fetched 2026-11-02). Newen shows it in
Settings > System > Licenses, as its condition 4 asks of software that uses the binary.
Newen never ships the binary: it downloads it from Cisco once the person allows it.
